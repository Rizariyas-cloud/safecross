Why SafeCross holds the light

1The camera misses people for short moments, especially wheelchair, walker and cane users.
2A person lost in the middle of the crossing is kept as uncertain until they could have finished: distance left ÷ 0.5 m/s × 1.3.
3In 279 simulated crossings using our real camera's mistakes, only 0.7% of mobility-aid users were still on the road at red, for about 1.7 s more green.

How an answer is made

1Knowledge graph: the decision log becomes numbered facts (G1, G2 …).
2Retrieval: only the facts about the question are selected.
3Explainer: our fine-tuned model answers and cites fact numbers, or says it cannot determine.
4Checker: rules confirm the cited facts support the answer; otherwise it is withheld.
In this browser prototype, answers are produced by rules over the same facts; the fine-tuned model runs in the full application.

About this prototype

SafeCross is a university research prototype (UOWD MAAI901 × MAAI902). It is not an official RTA or government product. Signal behaviour is simulated; camera accuracy figures come from the public PMMA dataset; images on the overview are illustrative.
